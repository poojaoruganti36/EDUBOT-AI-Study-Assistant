# French Revolution

The French Revolution began in **1789** and changed the political structure of France.

## Causes

- Social inequality between the three estates
- Heavy taxation on the common people
- Economic crisis and rising bread prices
- Influence of Enlightenment ideas

## Key Events

1. **Storming of the Bastille**
   This became a symbol of resistance against royal authority.
2. **Declaration of the Rights of Man and of the Citizen**
   It promoted liberty and equality.
3. **Execution of Louis XVI**
   This marked a major turning point in the revolution.
4. **Reign of Terror**
   Thousands were executed under suspicion of opposing the revolution.

## Outcomes

- End of feudal privileges
- Rise of republican ideas
- Political instability followed by Napoleon's rise
