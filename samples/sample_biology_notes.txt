Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make food using sunlight, carbon dioxide, and water.

Important points:
- It takes place mainly in chloroplasts.
- Chlorophyll captures light energy.
- Glucose is produced as food.
- Oxygen is released into the air.

Stages:
1. Light-dependent reactions
These reactions capture sunlight and produce ATP and NADPH. Water is split, releasing oxygen.

2. Calvin cycle
This stage uses carbon dioxide, ATP, and NADPH to produce glucose.

Why it matters:
- It supports food chains.
- It provides oxygen for life.
- It helps regulate atmospheric carbon dioxide.
